History of Luneta Park

Luneta Park, also known as Rizal Park, is one of the most important historical landmarks in the Philippines. It honors the life and sacrifice of Dr. Jose Rizal.

During the Spanish colonial period, the area was known as Bagumbayan. The name Luneta came from the Spanish-era use of the word lunette, referring to a crescent-shaped fortification. During the 1800s, the area became a popular gathering place for wealthy Spanish residents and Manila's upper class. People visited to enjoy evening walks, music, and social activities. However, it also became an execution ground where Spanish authorities executed Filipino rebels and individuals accused of opposing colonial rule.

Jose Rizal

Dr. Jose Rizal was a Filipino hero whose writings inspired people to fight for freedom and change. His monument stands proudly in Luneta Park.

Past and Present

Luneta Park has changed over time, but it continues to remind Filipinos of their history and the importance of protecting their heritage.

A Place for Everyone

Families, students, and visitors come to Luneta Park to relax, learn about history, and enjoy the open spaces.

Preserving Our Heritage

We can preserve Luneta Park by keeping it clean, respecting historical monuments, and learning about the people who shaped our country.
